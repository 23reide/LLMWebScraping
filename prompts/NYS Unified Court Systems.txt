NYS Unified Court Systems -County Filing and JDLS Search
https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/webccos/newyorkcc/countyFilingSearch
We check Individuals and Corporations to see if there are any outstanding NYS Commissioner of Labor judgments associates with the subcontractor, its officers, and companies owned by the officers.

1. Navigate to the official website.
2. Inspect the page and wait for the search form to load.
3. If the page is blank, wait 3 seconds and inspect it again.
4. If the page is still blank, stop and return status "error".
   Do not repeatedly reload the same blank page.
5. Enter the exact company name provided above in the Name field.
   Leave every other search option as it is.
6. Do NOT shorten the name, remove legal suffixes,
   try alternative spellings, or run additional searches.
7. Click the "I am a human" verification checkbox and wait 20 seconds
   for it to show as verified. Wait until the verification finishes then continue.
8. If an image puzzle or other challenge appears instead of a simple
   checkbox, stop and return status "error" with the note
   "verification challenge". Do not try to solve it. 
9. Click the actual Search button to submit the form.
10. Wait for the results page to load.
11. Verify that the results page actually appeared.
12. Do not claim that pages were reviewed unless you actually inspected them.
13. If the site shows a message that nothing was found, quote it exactly
    in page_confirmation.
