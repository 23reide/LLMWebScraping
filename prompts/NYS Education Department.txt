NYS Education Department, Office of the Professions - Verification Search 
https://eservices.nysed.gov/professions/verification-search
(Used to determine if a business or individual is authorized to do business in NYS providing the following services: Architecture, Engineers, Land Surveying, Landscape Architecture, Geology authorized to do business in NYS)  

We select “Business Entity” in the Search by option
We enter the name of the subcontractor in the “Profession” search box. 

For individual licenses (especially Architects) we select “Licensee Name” in the Search by option and “Architect” in the Profession search box, then enter the individual name in the box below.
