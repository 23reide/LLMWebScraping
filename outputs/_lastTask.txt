Websites:

NYS Department of State NYSDOL Debarments
https://apps.labor.ny.gov/EDList/searchPage.do
 (search by name of subcontractor, subsidiary/affiliated companies and companies owned by officers) 
There are 2 debarment lists here to search 
-DOL (Department of Labor prevailing wage 5-year debarments) and 
-WCB (Workers Compensation Board 1-year debarments).  
We usually check using Employer Name or EIN (however employer name gives us a broader search result).

NYS Department of State UCC Search
https://ucc-efiling.dos.ny.gov/
(search by name of subcontractor, its officers listed in Passport and affiliated/subsidiary companies, companies owned by officers)
We currently query this website using the Lien Search option in the Additional Options section and search by Debtor Name.
In the Advance Search Options section, we select Lien Type: Federal Tax Lien and leave all other options as is (copies of all lien and release must be downloaded and saved).

NYS Department of Taxation and Finance
https://uccfilings.dos.ny.gov/UCCSTW/
(search by names of subcontractor, its officers, subsidiary/affiliate, other companies owned by the officers of the subcontractor)
Are there any outstanding NYS DTF tax warrants. (We leave the search option as listed, searching by Base Word and Open Warrant Notice Status.)
If a name is listed, the record must be selected to open the search result.

NYS Incorporation
https://apps.dos.ny.gov/publicInquiry/
(search by subcontractor's name)
Is the subcontractor incorporated in the state or certified to do business in NYS. Is the incorporation currently active. Who is listed as the incorporator and is that individual one of the officers listed in Passport.

NYS Unified Court Systems -County Filing and JDLS Search
https://iapps.courts.state.ny.us/webccos/newyorkcc/countyFilingSearch
We check Individuals and Corporations to see if there are any outstanding NYS Commissioner of Labor judgments associates with the subcontractor, its officers, and companies owned by the officers.
